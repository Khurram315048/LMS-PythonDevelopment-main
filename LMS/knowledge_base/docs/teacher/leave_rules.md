# Teacher Leave Rules

A teacher is entitled to 10 casual leaves in one calendar year.

Planned leave must be applied for at least 2 days in advance to the head of department. The teacher must arrange a substitute or a make-up lecture for every class missed.

## Medical leave

Medical leave needs a medical certificate, submitted within 3 days of returning to work. Medical leave is separate from casual leave.

## Unpaid leave

Leave beyond the available balance is unpaid and the salary is reduced for those days.
