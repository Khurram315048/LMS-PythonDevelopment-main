# Marks, Assignments and Quizzes

The marks of a course are divided as assignments and quizzes 20 percent, midterm 30 percent and final 50 percent.

Assignments and quizzes must be reviewed within 7 days of the student upload. A reviewed submission gets marks and a rating.

Midterm and final marks must be entered within 7 days of the exam. Marks cannot be changed after the result is published without the approval of the head of department.

A student who disputes marks must contact the course teacher within 7 days of the result. The teacher checks the paper and informs the department of any change.
