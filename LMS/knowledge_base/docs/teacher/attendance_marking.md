# Attendance Marking for Teachers

The course teacher marks attendance for every lecture on the same day. Each student is marked Present or Absent.

A wrong attendance entry can be corrected within 3 days of the lecture. After 3 days a correction needs the approval of the head of department.

A student with attendance below 75 percent in a course may not be allowed to sit in the final exam. Teachers should warn such students before the midterm.

Medical or emergency absence is reported to the teacher with proof within 7 days. The decision to excuse the absence is taken by the department.
