# Teacher Salary Rules

Salary is paid by the 5th of every month for the previous month.

The monthly salary has a basic salary, a bonus if approved, and deductions. Net salary is basic salary plus bonus minus deductions.

Deductions are made for unpaid leave and for any approved advance or tax.

A salary record can show the status pending or paid. If a salary is still pending after the 10th of the month, contact the accounts office.

A wrong amount in a salary record must be reported to the accounts office within 7 days.
