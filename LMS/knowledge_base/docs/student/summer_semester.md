# Summer Semester

The summer semester is a short semester offered after the regular spring semester. It is mainly for retaking failed courses and for improvement.

Applications are open only while the summer application window is open. The dates are announced by the office. Check the Summer Semester page of the portal to see if applications are currently open.

Apply from the Summer Semester page by selecting the courses you need. A student can register for a maximum of 2 courses in one summer semester.

Summer semester fee is separate from the regular semester fee and is charged per course.

Attendance rules in the summer semester are the same as in the regular semester: minimum 75 percent.
