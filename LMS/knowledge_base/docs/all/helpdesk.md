# Help Desk

The Help Desk is the first place to go when you have a problem with the LMS portal or with an office service.

Office hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM. The office is closed on Saturday, Sunday and public holidays.

For a problem with your account, password or login, contact the IT office during office hours.

For a wrong attendance entry, a wrong marks entry or a wrong grade, first contact the teacher of that course. If the teacher cannot fix it, submit a complaint from the Complaints page of the portal.

For fee problems, such as a paid voucher still showing as due, contact the accounts office with your voucher copy.

You can submit complaints and suggestions from the Complaints page. You can see the status of each complaint there: Pending, Solved or Rejected.
