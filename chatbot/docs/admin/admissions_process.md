# Admission Process

Admissions are offered once a year for each program. The office announces the dates, the eligibility and the merit criteria.

A candidate must submit the application form, the last qualification documents and the admission fee voucher.

After the merit list is published, selected students complete their registration at the office within 7 days. A student who does not complete registration in time loses the seat.

A program coordinator is assigned to each program. The coordinator guides the students of that program.
