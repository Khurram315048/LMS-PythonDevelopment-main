# Fee Collection and Defaulters

Fee vouchers are generated every month and are due by the 10th of the month. Fee paid after the 10th may be charged a late fee.

When a student uploads a paid voucher, the accounts office verifies it within 3 working days and then marks the month as paid.

A student with unpaid dues can be blocked from exams and results until the dues are cleared. The accounts office keeps the list of defaulters and updates it every week.

A fee dispute is reviewed by the accounts office with the voucher copy.
