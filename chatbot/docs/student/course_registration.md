# Course Registration

Course registration happens at the start of every semester. It is possible only while the registration window is open. The office announces the dates.

Register from the Course Registration page of the portal. The page shows only the courses you are eligible for. Eligibility depends on your current semester, the prerequisites you have passed and your result record.

A student can register for a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 6 courses in one regular semester.

Registration cannot be changed after the window closes. If you have a problem, contact the office before the deadline.

A student with unpaid fee dues or a frozen semester cannot register for courses.
