# Semester Freeze

Semester freeze means pausing your studies for one semester. You keep your admission and continue from the same semester when you return.

A student can apply for a semester freeze from the Semester Freeze page of the portal before the semester registration deadline.

The application status is Pending until the department reviews it. After review it becomes Approved or Rejected. Review usually takes 5 to 7 working days.

While your semester is frozen you cannot use student services such as attendance, assignment upload or course registration for that semester. You need to contact the office to return.

A freeze cannot be taken in the middle of exams. A student can freeze a maximum of 2 semesters during the whole degree.
