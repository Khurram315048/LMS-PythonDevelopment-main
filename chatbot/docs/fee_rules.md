# Fee Rules

Fee is charged every month. The fee status of each month is either paid or due.

The monthly fee must be paid by the 10th of the month. Fee paid after the 10th may be charged a late fee.

To pay, collect the fee voucher, pay it at the designated bank, and then upload a clear photo of the paid voucher on the Fee page of the portal.

After you upload the voucher, the status can still show as due. The accounts office checks the voucher and then marks the month as paid. This can take 2 to 3 working days.

If your voucher is paid and uploaded but the status is still due after 3 working days, contact the accounts office with your voucher copy.

A student with unpaid dues may not be allowed to sit in exams or receive results until the dues are cleared.
