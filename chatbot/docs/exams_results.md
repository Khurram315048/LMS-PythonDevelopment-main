# Exams, Results and Grading

Each semester has two main exam categories: Midterm and Final. The exam date, time, location and mode are shown in the exam schedule of the portal.

Marks are divided as: Assignments and Quizzes 20 percent, Midterm 30 percent, Final 50 percent.

Grading scale: A is 85 percent and above, B is 70 to 84 percent, C is 60 to 69 percent, D is 50 to 59 percent, and F (fail) is below 50 percent.

The minimum passing grade in a course is D. A student with grade F must retake the course.

Semester GPA is calculated from the subject GPA of all courses of that semester. CGPA is calculated over all semesters.

Results are published on the portal after the final exams. If you think your marks are wrong, contact the course teacher within 7 days of the result.
