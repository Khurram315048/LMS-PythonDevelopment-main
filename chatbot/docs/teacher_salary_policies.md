# Teacher Hiring Policy (DUMMY sample, replace with the real policy)

Eligibility: A candidate must hold at least a Bachelor degree in a relevant field with minimum 16 years of education. Master degree is preferred for teaching BS programs. PhD is required to supervise MS or PhD students.

Required documents: Candidates must submit CV, copies of all degrees and transcripts, CNIC copy, two recent passport size photographs, experience letters (if any) and two references.

Hiring process: Step 1 is application to the HR office. Step 2 is shortlisting by the head of department. Step 3 is a demo lecture. Step 4 is an interview by a panel of the head of department and one senior faculty member. Step 5 is the final decision by the HR office.

Timeline: The full hiring process normally takes 3 to 4 weeks from the application deadline.

Probation: Every new teacher is on probation for 6 months. Performance is reviewed at the end of the probation period. After a satisfactory review the teacher is confirmed as a permanent member of the faculty.

Contract and notice period: The appointment letter states the salary, designation and joining date. A confirmed teacher must give 30 days written notice to resign. A teacher on probation must give 15 days written notice.

Course assignment: Courses are assigned by the head of department according to the teacher's qualification and experience. A teacher normally teaches 3 to 4 courses per semester.

Visiting faculty: Visiting teachers are hired per semester on a per-lecture basis. They are not covered by the leave and annual increment rules.

Contact: For hiring questions, contact the HR office. This assistant cannot change, approve or reject any application.
