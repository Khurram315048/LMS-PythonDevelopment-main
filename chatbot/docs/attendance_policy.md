# Attendance Policy

Attendance is marked by the course teacher for every lecture. The status of each lecture is Present or Absent.

The minimum required attendance is 75 percent in every course. Attendance is calculated per course, not as one overall number.

A student whose attendance in a course is below 75 percent may not be allowed to sit in the final exam of that course. The final decision is taken by the department.

Medical or emergency absence must be reported to the course teacher and the office with proof within 7 days of returning. Approved absence may be considered by the department, but it is not automatic.

You can check your attendance from the Attendance page of the portal. If an entry is wrong, contact the course teacher first.
